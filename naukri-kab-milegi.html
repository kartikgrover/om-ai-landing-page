When Will I Get a Job? — The Vedic Answer

"Naukri kab milegi" — when will I get a job — is the biggest career question there is, and Vedic astrology has a real answer. Job timing in your chart is decided by the interaction of five things: the 10th house and its lord, Saturn, Jupiter, your active Vimshottari Dasha, and current transits. When these align, the job comes.

The Short Answer

AI job prediction can narrow your job window to a specific period of a few months — not an exact date. The window sits at the intersection of your active Mahadasha/Antardasha and the transit of Jupiter or Saturn over your 10th house or its lord. When the Dasha changes, a stuck job search often starts moving suddenly — that is why timing matters.

The 5 Things Vedic AI Looks At

Every accurate career prediction comes from these five signals. The AI weighs all of them together.

1. The 10th House

The house of karma and career. Its lord, occupants, and the aspects falling on it reveal whether the job comes early, how growth unfolds, and which field is indicated.

2. Saturn (Shani)

The natural significator of work and service. A strong Saturn gives a stable job and disciplined growth; a weak or afflicted Saturn brings struggle and delay. Saturn's Dasha-Antardasha is a major career trigger.

3. Jupiter (Guru)

The significator of expansion and opportunity. Jupiter's transit over your 10th house or its lord is the classical time for a new job, promotion, or better offer — once every 12-year cycle.

4. Dasha Period

Your Vimshottari Dasha decides when chart promises activate. Career yogas peak in the Dasha/Antardasha of the 10th lord, Saturn, the Sun, or Mercury.

5. Dashamsa (D10)

The divisional chart of career. The D10 Lagna, its 10th house, and planet placements show how strong your D1 career promises really are and in which field success comes.

+ Transits (Gochar)

Current planetary movements relative to your natal chart. Jupiter and Saturn transits over the 10th house or its lord are the biggest real-time triggers — for jobs, promotions, and transfers alike.

Sarkari Naukri Yog, Delay Signals & Job Change

Whether your chart has government job yog, why the delay is happening, when to switch — the AI checks all of it in your Kundli.

Sarkari Naukri Yog

The Sun is the significator of authority and government — a strong Sun connected to the 10th house or its lord is the biggest sign of a government job. Add a well-placed Saturn (service) and a favorable Sun-Moon combination, and the Sarkari yog gets strong. The AI tells you how strong the yog is and in which Dasha it delivers.

Saturn on the 10th House

Saturn's heavy aspect on or transit through the 10th house can slow the job down — but Saturn delays, it does not deny. Saturn-delayed jobs, once they arrive, are often the most stable and long-lasting. The AI tells you when Saturn's weight lifts.

Weak or Afflicted 10th Lord

A debilitated or combust 10th lord, or one hemmed in by challenging planets, can signal career struggle and repeated interview setbacks. The AI checks whether Neechabhanga or a strong D10 placement rescues it — and when the favorable Dasha arrives.

Rahu/Ketu in the 10th

Rahu in the 10th can pull toward unconventional careers — technology, media, foreign companies; Ketu signals repeated changes of direction. Both are timing-sensitive — the breakthrough often comes at a specific Dasha activation.
