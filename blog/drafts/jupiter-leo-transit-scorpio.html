Jupiter Transit in Leo 2026&ndash;27 for Scorpio Ascendant (10th House)

The 10th rewards groundwork already laid. Jupiter here is less a door opening than a ceiling lifting on work you have already been doing. Jupiter enters Leo on October 31, 2026, which for Scorpio ascendant is your 10th house — career, standing, and public life. It does not stay put: Jupiter turns retrograde in Leo on December 13, 2026, slips back into Cancer on January 25, 2027, and only returns to Leo for good on June 26, 2027, finally leaving on November 27, 2027. Most write-ups miss the return leg, which is why this transit is felt in two waves rather than one.

Transit Overview

Jupiter is the natural significator of wisdom, expansion, and dharma. Leo is ruled by the Sun — Jupiter's ally — so Jupiter sits here as a welcome guest rather than in its own or its exaltation sign. Its results are traditionally read as warm and visible, but less overwhelming than the Cancer exaltation that preceded them.

For Scorpio ascendant, Jupiter is a functional benefic — it owns houses that work in your favour, so its transit results are traditionally read at full strength. Jupiter rules your 2nd and 5th houses, which means the affairs of those houses are carried into your 10th house for the duration. That combination — not Jupiter alone — is what shapes the result.

What 10th House Jupiter Means

The 10th house governs profession, authority, reputation, and karma in the sense of visible action. BPHS lists royalty, place, profession, honour, and father among its indications. Jupiter transiting the 10th is felt in public rather than in private.

Promotion, recognition, and a broadening of professional scope are the classical readings. The nuance is that the 10th is an upachaya — it grows with effort — so this transit is believed to reward work already done rather than manufacture opportunity from nothing.

Key Areas of Impact

  • Career growth: Promotion and expanded responsibility are the classical signatures. Existing groundwork matters.
  • Reputation: Public standing rises. Traditionally a good window to be visible.
  • Authority: Relations with seniors and institutions soften; approvals come more easily.
  • New role: Job change undertaken now is traditionally considered well-timed.
  • Ethical work: The 10th under Jupiter is believed to favour work with a defensible purpose over purely lucrative work.
  • Workload: The caution: Jupiter expands. Scope creep is the common complaint of this transit.

Where Jupiter's Aspects Land

Jupiter is unusual among the planets in casting three full aspects — on the 5th, 7th and 9th houses from wherever it sits (BPHS Ch.26). Sitting in your 10th house, it therefore also influences your 2nd, 4th, 6th houses for the whole transit.

In practice this matters more than the placement itself for many charts: a house Jupiter merely aspects still receives its protection. For Scorpio ascendant that means 2nd house matters (wealth, family, and speech), 4th house matters (home, mother, and inner peace), 6th house matters (health, service, and obstacles) are all traditionally supported alongside the 10th house itself.

Timing & Phases

The transit runs in two waves with a Cancer interlude between them. The dates below are computed from the sidereal ephemeris, not approximated:

  • October 31, 2026 – December 13, 2026 (direct): Recognition arrives first, usually for work already completed.
  • December 13, 2026 – January 25, 2027 (retrograde, still Leo): Retrograde is the negotiation window — revisit a title, a scope, or a salary rather than change employer.
  • January 25, 2027 – June 26, 2027 (back in Cancer, your 9th): Jupiter falls to the 9th, which is its own best house. Fortune improves while career pauses.
  • June 26, 2027 – November 27, 2027 (direct, final pass): The consolidating pass. Promotions taken here are traditionally read as durable.

How to Navigate This Transit

  • Ask for the promotion: Recognition is available but rarely volunteered. The 10th is an upachaya — it responds to initiative.
  • Change roles deliberately: Job moves under this transit are traditionally well-timed. Use a direct phase.
  • Guard the scope: Jupiter expands the job as fast as the title. Agree boundaries in writing.
  • Choose defensible work: 10th-house Jupiter is classically said to favour work with a purpose over work with only a payslip.
  • Read your dasha first: a transit is the weather; the dasha decides whether you are positioned to use it. Classical practice reads dasha first, transit second.
  • Do not coast: Jupiter is traditionally said to open doors, not walk through them.
