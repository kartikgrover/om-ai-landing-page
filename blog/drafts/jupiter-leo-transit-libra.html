Jupiter Transit in Leo 2026&ndash;27 for Libra Ascendant (11th House)

If you only track one Jupiter transit for money, this is the one — the 11th is the house of gains, and a benefic here is the most reliably profitable pairing in the cycle. Jupiter enters Leo on October 31, 2026, which for Libra ascendant is your 11th house — gains, networks, and elder siblings. It does not stay put: Jupiter turns retrograde in Leo on December 13, 2026, slips back into Cancer on January 25, 2027, and only returns to Leo for good on June 26, 2027, finally leaving on November 27, 2027. Most write-ups miss the return leg, which is why this transit is felt in two waves rather than one.

Transit Overview

Jupiter is the natural significator of wisdom, expansion, and dharma. Leo is ruled by the Sun — Jupiter's ally — so Jupiter sits here as a welcome guest rather than in its own or its exaltation sign. Its results are traditionally read as warm and visible, but less overwhelming than the Cancer exaltation that preceded them.

For Libra ascendant, Jupiter is a functional malefic — it owns houses that complicate rather than assist, so classical practice is to read its transit results with more caution than the general reputation of Jupiter suggests. Jupiter rules your 3rd and 6th houses, which means the affairs of those houses are carried into your 11th house for the duration. That combination — not Jupiter alone — is what shapes the result.

What 11th House Jupiter Means

The 11th house governs income, gains, fulfilment of desire, social networks, and elder siblings. It is an upachaya house and traditionally the most reliably profitable of the twelve for a transiting benefic.

Jupiter here is read as a straightforward gain transit — income rises, the right people appear, and long-held wishes find a route. The classical caution is that the 11th also expands desire itself, so satisfaction does not always keep pace with income.

Key Areas of Impact

  • Income: One of the most dependable money transits in the cycle. Multiple income streams are common.
  • Networks: Influential contacts arrive, often without being sought. Traditionally worth cultivating deliberately.
  • Long-held wishes: The 11th is the house of desire fulfilled; stalled ambitions tend to move.
  • Elder siblings: Their circumstances traditionally improve; relations warm.
  • Groups: Community, association, and collective work are favoured.
  • Appetite: The caution: the 11th expands wanting as fast as having. Define enough in advance.

Where Jupiter's Aspects Land

Jupiter is unusual among the planets in casting three full aspects — on the 5th, 7th and 9th houses from wherever it sits (BPHS Ch.26). Sitting in your 11th house, it therefore also influences your 3rd, 5th, 7th houses for the whole transit.

In practice this matters more than the placement itself for many charts: a house Jupiter merely aspects still receives its protection. For Libra ascendant that means 3rd house matters (courage, siblings, and effort), 5th house matters (children, intellect, and past merit), 7th house matters (marriage, partnership, and trade) are all traditionally supported alongside the 11th house itself.

Timing & Phases

The transit runs in two waves with a Cancer interlude between them. The dates below are computed from the sidereal ephemeris, not approximated:

  • October 31, 2026 – December 13, 2026 (direct): Income and useful contacts arrive early. This is the most immediately visible of the twelve.
  • December 13, 2026 – January 25, 2027 (retrograde, still Leo): Retrograde converts new gains into recurring ones. A good stretch to formalise an income stream.
  • January 25, 2027 – June 26, 2027 (back in Cancer, your 10th): Jupiter drops to the 10th. Gains pause; standing and responsibility grow instead.
  • June 26, 2027 – November 27, 2027 (direct, final pass): The profitable pass. Long-held ambitions traditionally resolve in this stretch.

How to Navigate This Transit

  • Formalise the income: Convert a one-off gain into something recurring — the retrograde is the window for it.
  • Cultivate the network: Contacts arrive unbidden. Traditional practice says follow up deliberately rather than wait.
  • Name your enough: The 11th expands wanting as fast as having. Decide the number before the transit does.
  • Revive a stalled ambition: The house of desire fulfilled. Old plans are traditionally easiest to restart here.
  • Read your dasha first: a transit is the weather; the dasha decides whether you are positioned to use it. Classical practice reads dasha first, transit second.
  • Temper it: Jupiter is a functional malefic for Libra ascendant, so the popular “Jupiter fixes everything” framing does not apply cleanly. Read these as openings that still need work.
