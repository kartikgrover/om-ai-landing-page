How your 2027 months are found

  1. Your chart. Your birth details set your lagna (rising sign) and the 12 houses. The lagna changes about every two hours, so the birth time matters.
  2. One house for each area. Marriage is read from the 7th house, career from the 10th, money from the 2nd and 11th, and a home from the 4th.
  3. Dasha and transits. A window opens when your running dasha (planetary period) connects to that house, or when the planets’ transits support it. Strongest marks the months where both happen together.
  4. The reason. Every window comes from your own chart. Ask Om.AI why, and it explains from your chart. Your first question is free.

Frequently asked questions

What does “Your 2027” show?

The months of 2027 your birth chart opens for four areas: marriage, career, money and a home. It reads your dasha and the planets’ transits against the house for each area. It’s free and needs no sign-up.

Why does a window start in 2026 or run into 2028?

Dashas and transits don’t follow the calendar. A window that touches 2027 is shown in full, from the month it opens to the month it closes.

What does “Strongest” mean?

In those months your running dasha connects to the area’s house and the transits support it too. Windows without the mark rest on one of the two.

Why don’t I see marriage dates?

Marriage timing is shown only for people 18 or older. If you’re 18 or older and the card shows no clear window, your chart doesn’t show one in 2027.

Do I need my exact birth time?

As close as you can get it. The lagna changes about every two hours and every house moves with it, so a wrong time can move the windows. If you’re unsure, check a birth certificate or hospital record.

How do I ask why?

Tap Ask why on any card. On a phone, install Om.AI and ask the question shown as your first one; it’s free. On a computer, ask it in your browser.
