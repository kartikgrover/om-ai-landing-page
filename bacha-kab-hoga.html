Bacha Kab Hoga? — The Vedic Answer

"Bacha kab hoga" — when will we have a child — is one of the most tender questions a family asks, and Vedic astrology has read it for centuries. Child timing in your chart is set by the meeting of five things: the 5th house (santan bhava) and its lord, Jupiter, your active Vimshottari Dasha, the 9th house, and current transits. When they align, that is your favourable period.

The Short Answer

The AI can narrow your favourable timing to a specific window of a few months — not an exact date, and never a fixed promise. The window sits at the intersection of your active Mahadasha/Antardasha and the Jupiter–Saturn transit over your 5th house. When the Dasha changes, a waiting yog often opens — which is why timing matters.

The 5 Things Vedic AI Looks At

Every reliable reading of child timing comes from these five signals. The AI weighs all of them together.

1. The 5th House

Santan bhava — the house of children. Its lord, occupants, and the aspects falling on it show how strong the promise is and where the support comes from.

2. The 5th Lord

The planet ruling your 5th house. Its strength and its connection with the Lagna lord are the foundation — and its Dasha-Antardasha is the biggest timing trigger.

3. Jupiter (Guru)

The natural significator of children. Classical texts also read the 5th house counted from Jupiter. Jupiter's transit over your 5th house or its lord is the traditional favourable time.

4. Dasha Period

Your Vimshottari Dasha decides when chart promises activate. The Dasha or Antardasha of the 5th lord, Jupiter, or the 9th lord is when the timing peaks.

5. The 9th House

Fortune and blessings — and the 5th counted from the 5th. The classical progeny combinations pair the 9th with the 5th again and again; the 9th lord's periods count as supportive.

+ Transits (Gochar)

Current planetary movements relative to your natal chart. Jupiter and Saturn influencing your 5th house together — the double transit — is the biggest real-time signal.

Santan Yog, Delay Signals & Both Charts

Whether your chart carries Santan Yog, why a delay shows up, and when the favourable period begins — the AI checks all of it in your Kundli.

Santan Yog

A strong 5th lord, Jupiter's aspect on the 5th house, and a friendly link between the 5th lord and the Lagna lord — these are the classic signals of Santan Yog. The AI tells you how strong the yog is in your chart and which Dasha brings it to life.

Saturn's Influence

Saturn's heavy aspect on the 5th house or its lord can slow the timing — but Saturn delays, it does not deny. Delayed yogs typically open the moment a favourable Dasha begins. The AI tells you when the influence lightens.

Rahu/Ketu in the 5th

Rahu or Ketu in the 5th house makes the matter timing-sensitive — the promise is there, but it opens at a specific Dasha activation. The AI finds that turning point in your Dasha timeline.

Both Charts Matter

Tradition reads child timing from both partners' charts. This calculator reads one chart — in the Om.AI app you can add your partner's chart with compatibility and ask about the full picture together.

What is Santan Yog and how do I check it in my kundli?

Santan Yog is the combination where the 5th house, its lord, and Jupiter are all strong and well placed. A well-placed 5th lord connected to the Lagna lord, Jupiter's aspect on the 5th house, and supportive planets in the 5th are the classic signals. Why is there a delay? What does the chart show?

Heavy Saturn influence on the 5th house or its lord, a weak or afflicted 5th lord, Rahu-Ketu in the 5th, or an unsupportive Dasha are the common astrological delay signals. Saturn delays — it does not deny. The delay typically ends when a favourable Dasha begins or Jupiter transits your 5th house. Do I need an exact birth time?
