When does Shani Dhaiya end?

The short answer

From 29 Mar 2025 to 23 Feb 2028, Dhaiya runs for two Moon signs: Ashtama Shani for Leo (Singh) and Kantaka Shani for Sagittarius (Dhanu), with a break from 3 Jun to 20 Oct 2027. The next Dhaiya begins on 3 Jun 2027 for Virgo (Kanya, Ashtama Shani) and Capricorn (Makar, Kantaka Shani) and ends on 17 Apr 2030.

Dhaiya is set by your Moon sign, not your Sun sign, so everyone with the same Moon sign shares the same dates. Don't know your Moon sign? Use the calculator above. If you do, find it in the table below.

How the dates are counted

A Dhaiya starts the day Saturn first enters the 4th or 8th sign from your Moon, and ends the day Saturn leaves that sign for the last time. If a retrograde Saturn slips back into the previous sign for a few months, or steps into the next one and returns, that is a break inside the same Dhaiya, not a new one. These are the same dates the Om.AI app uses when it answers your Saturn questions.

What Shani Dhaiya is

In Vedic astrology, Saturn's transit is read from the sign your Moon was in at birth (Phaladeepika, Ch. 26, Sl. 1). Saturn spends about two and a half years in each sign, and "dhaiya" means two and a half. When the sign Saturn is in is the 4th or the 8th from your Moon sign, that stretch is your Dhaiya.

Kantaka and Ashtama Shani

Saturn in the 4th sign from the Moon is called Kantaka Shani; Kantaka is the classical name for the 1st, 4th, 7th and 10th houses (Brihat Jataka, Ch. 1, Sl. 17). Saturn in the 8th sign from the Moon is Ashtama Shani. Both are Dhaiya; only the house differs.

What the classical texts say

The transit chapter of Brihat Samhita links Saturn in the 4th from the Moon with distance from friends, money and spouse, and a restless mind (Ch. 104, Sl. 42), and Saturn in the 8th with distance from spouse and children and tiring journeys (Sl. 44). The same chapter counts Saturn in the 3rd, 6th and 11th from the Moon as good (Sl. 4).

Where the name comes from

The classical texts we work from (Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Brihat Jataka, Phaladeepika, Brihat Samhita) don't use the word Dhaiya. It is a later, popular name built on these transit verses. We take the dates from Saturn's actual sign changes rather than adding two and a half years, so some Dhaiyas run shorter than that and some longer.

How much of it you feel

These verses describe the transit for everyone with your Moon sign. How it shows up in one life depends on the rest of the chart: the dasha that is running, which house Saturn crosses from your lagna, and which houses Saturn rules for you. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (Ch. 34) counts Saturn as a helpful planet for Taurus, Libra and Aquarius lagna. Hard years are real for many people, and they end; that is why the dates matter.

Dhaiya and Sade Sati: the difference

In Sade Sati, Saturn crosses three signs in a row: the 12th from your Moon, your Moon's own sign and the 2nd from it, about seven and a half years in all. Dhaiya is one sign, the 4th or the 8th from your Moon, about two and a half years. So the two never run at the same time. In each Saturn cycle of about 30 years, every Moon sign has one Sade Sati and two Dhaiyas.

Frequently asked questions

What is Shani Dhaiya?

Shani Dhaiya is the time Saturn spends in the 4th or the 8th sign from your Moon sign. Saturn stays about two and a half years in a sign, which is where the name (dhaiya, two and a half) comes from. In the 4th sign it is called Kantaka Shani, in the 8th Ashtama Shani. It is counted from your Moon sign, not your Sun sign or lagna.

Which signs have Shani Dhaiya in 2026?

Leo (Singh) and Sagittarius (Dhanu). Leo has Ashtama Shani and Sagittarius has Kantaka Shani, both from 29 Mar 2025 to 23 Feb 2028, while Saturn is in Pisces. Saturn moves into Aries from 3 Jun to 20 Oct 2027, so both have a break in those months.

When will Ashtama Shani end for Leo (Simha)?

On 23 Feb 2028, the day Saturn leaves Pisces for good. Kantaka Shani for Sagittarius ends the same day. The next Dhaiya, Ashtama Shani for Virgo and Kantaka Shani for Capricorn, runs from 3 Jun 2027 to 17 Apr 2030, with breaks from 20 Oct 2027 to 23 Feb 2028 and from 8 Aug to 5 Oct 2029. Every other sign is in the table.

Is Dhaiya counted from the Moon sign or the lagna?

From the Moon sign. Vedic astrology reads transits from the natal Moon (Phaladeepika, Ch. 26, Sl. 1), so everyone with the same Moon sign has the same Dhaiya dates, whatever their lagna. The lagna decides which house of your chart Saturn crosses, so it matters when reading the effects.

What is the difference between Dhaiya and Sade Sati?

In Sade Sati, Saturn crosses the 12th sign from your Moon, the Moon sign itself and the 2nd from it, about seven and a half years in all. Dhaiya is a single sign, the 4th or the 8th from your Moon, about two and a half years. The two never overlap. Check your Sade Sati dates with the Sade Sati calculator.

Why does my Dhaiya have a break?

Saturn is retrograde for about four and a half months every year, and near a sign boundary it slips back into the previous sign. Saturn enters Aries on 3 Jun 2027, returns to Pisces on 20 Oct 2027 and enters Aries again on 23 Feb 2028. The Dhaiya pauses in those months, but it is the same Dhaiya, not a new one.

Is Shani Dhaiya always a bad time?

Not for everyone. Brihat Samhita describes Saturn in the 4th and 8th from the Moon as a hard transit (Ch. 104, Sl. 42 and 44), and many people do feel it that way. How much it shows up in one life depends on the whole chart: the running dasha, which house Saturn crosses from your lagna, and which houses Saturn rules for you. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra (Ch. 34) counts Saturn as a helpful planet for Taurus, Libra and Aquarius lagna. And every Dhaiya has a fixed end date, which the calculator above gives you.
