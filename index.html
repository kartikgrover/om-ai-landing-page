What is Om.AI?

Om.AI is an AI-powered Vedic astrology app for Android and iOS, and you can also use it in your browser. It calculates your birth chart (Kundli) from your exact birth date, time and place, then answers your questions from that chart instead of a generic sun sign. You can chat with the AI astrologer about career, marriage, love, health, money or timing; generate your Kundli with Lagna, Navamsa (D9) and Vimshottari Dasha; check marriage compatibility with Ashtakoot matching; see the best hours of your day; and read a daily Bhagavad Gita verse. The app is rated 4.8 stars, the interface is in English and Hindi, and the chat replies in whatever language you write in.

How does the AI astrology work?

It works in three steps. First, you enter your birth date, exact time of birth and city. Om.AI calculates your Kundli: your Lagna (ascendant), the nine Vedic planets (Navagraha) across the 12 houses, your Navamsa (D9) chart and your running Dasha periods. Second, it reads the chart using classical texts including Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Brihat Jataka and Phaladeepika, picking up yogas, doshas such as Manglik, and planetary strength. Third, when you ask a question, for example “When will I get married?” or “Is this a good time to change jobs?”, it combines your chart with your current Dasha and upcoming transits to give a specific answer, often with a time window. Every answer is specific to your chart, not a generic sun-sign horoscope.

What does the Compatibility feature do?

It compares two birth charts using the traditional Ashtakoot system, which scores eight areas: Varna, Vashya, Tara, Yoni, Graha Maitri, Gana, Bhakoot and Nadi. The maximum is 36 points, and 18 or more is traditionally considered favorable for marriage. Beyond the score, Om.AI looks at how the two charts interact (the aspects between your planets) and whether both partners' Dasha periods line up, and you can ask follow-up questions about the match in chat. Enter both partners' birth details to get the report.
